## Decisions needed for a live installation

The founder has not selected hosted or hospital-local deployment. Local engineering
continues without buying services. Until a choice is supplied, retain both as
installation targets; this does not imply offline synchronization or hybrid support.

Before external deployment, prepare the concrete topology, configuration, costs,
access plan and rollback procedure for approval. At that point the founder supplies
the hosting/hardware owner, domain or hospital DNS, budget and responsible operator.
Credentials must use private files or a secret store, never chat or Git.

Before live records, a pilot hospital must confirm department coverage, permissions,
clinical forms, laboratory/medicine/financial policy and downtime handling. Qualified
local review must settle data location, privacy and retention obligations. The
operator must demonstrate offsite backup access, independent key custody, measured
recovery and working alert escalation. These approvals cannot be generated by tests.
Use [the pilot workbook](hospital-pilot-acceptance.md) to record them privately.
