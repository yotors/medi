## Disposition

Retain the PRD as **draft/partial** and suitable for reversible UX, architecture preparation and synthetic work. Resolve the two medium and one low detail gaps before affected story acceptance is finalized; open clinical, commercial, deployment and regulatory decisions remain explicit release prerequisites rather than defects requiring invented answers. This reconciliation neither approves a live pilot nor changes the user's authorization for continued local development.
